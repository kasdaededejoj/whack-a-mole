# BRAND — Adam's perma-stealth, invite-only avant-streetwear brand

Single home for everything about the brand. Sections marked **[interview pending]** are still empty on purpose: do not fill them by guessing;
ask Adam. Last updated 2026-10-17. (Garment specs live elsewhere and are not part of this page.)

## 1. Identity
- Founder and fashion designer: Adam. Perma-stealth, invite-only avant-streetwear brand.
- Positioned between luxury stealth (The Row, Bottega Veneta) and avant-garde (Rick Owens, Julius, Yohji Yamamoto).
- A **taste institution**, not a fashion brand with content attached. Clothing is one artifact among music, archives, essays, a browser-based game and a Discord community.
- Three-axis archetype: **normality, mysticism, unconventionalism.** Always reference the full archetype in brand decisions. Bee orchid mimicry is one extension of it, not the defining concept: do not over-index on orchids.
- Common thread across all output: **curiosity.**

## 2. Ethos
- Two-stage arc: liberation from irrelevant social authorities, then arrival at banality (ordinary life with interior conviction), then deep appreciation.
- Direction is **centralised and founder-authored**; interpretation is **decentralised** to the audience. Editorial, non-audience-captured. The goal is cultivating perception.
- Every touchpoint should feel like a museum, an archive or an editorial publication.
- Game design philosophy: no tutorials, no explanations. "Let them discover it."
- **Adam's own words on the ethos (2026-10-17):** "we believe that everyone should embrace banality and unconventionalism." Whether you're into something no one knows about,
  or something seen as cringy, you should take part in it.
- **What "banality" means here:** *Das Man* (a German concept; Adam has not yet named the book). A person with different interests, some of which don't correlate,
  held for their own enjoyment. For the brand that covers darkness, art, interesting music, relics, history and anything outside the mainstream that is "shrouded"
  only because those who appreciate it value the effort that went into making it.
  *(CTP note: in Heidegger's* Being and Time*, das Man is the anonymous "they" of social conformity, close to the opposite of how it is used here. Adam to confirm the source and the intended sense.)*
- **Why stealth (Adam, personal):** he does not want the brand to be mainstream. It should be discovered, chanced upon, by a community that appreciates it. That is why the brand takes pride in nuance.

## 3. The open tension (raise it whenever brand infrastructure decisions come up; do not let it pass unexamined)
Is The Realm meant to **teach** taste or **filter** for it? If teach, every touchpoint (game, Discord, drops, archive) is an educational system.
If filter, they are marketing systems. This is the foundational architectural question and it is unresolved.
**Adam's current lean (2026-10-17): both, in sequence**: filter first (the funnel and the game), then teach inside (the Discord and what follows). Not final.

## 4. Language and voice (answered by Adam, 2026-10-17)
- **Tone:** cryptic and mystical.
- **Casing:** all lowercase in brand copy.
- **Address:** collective "we": the institution speaks as one body.
- **Never:** hype words (drop, exclusive, limited, grail, fire); emojis and exclamation marks; explaining itself (no tutorials, no "here's what this means"); selling language (buy now, shop, discount, sale, calls to action).

## 5. History
**Origin (Adam, 2026-10-17):** it started as a passion for clothes, and as a passion for being yourself after being told to conform to society and not stand out.
The aim is to let people be themselves, whether that is boring, unconventional or just different.
**Still [interview pending]:** the decisions that shaped the brand over time, and what was tried and rejected.

## 6. How we operate
**Funnel (multi-gate):** physical business cards -> password-protected Carrd page -> capped Discord invite links.
**Community:** the Discord is called "the Realm". A custom `discord.py` bot creates a private thread for each new member when the onboarded role is assigned,
posting an ambition question visible only to the member and the Founder role.
**Measurement:** Google Analytics on Carrd; invite-link batch tracking, to read conversion by drop context.
**Commerce and build:** Shopify stays as the backend for inventory and analytics. Framer is a build-and-freeze frontend before launch.
**IP strategy:** the registered logo is used privately (packaging, inserts, members-only assets); an unregistered logo is the public-facing mark.
**Research behind the brand:** stealth precedents (Margiela, Corteiz, The Row, Chrome Hearts); consumer-psychology frameworks (Bourdieu, Han/Nunes/Dreze, Makkar/Yap);
Singapore retail nodes and digital communities; guerrilla marketing mechanics (A6 posters, belly-band envelopes, cluster drop strategy).
**Drops:** small cluster drops, released through the Discord.
**Decisions:** Adam alone has the final say. AI assistants advise and offer options; they do not decide.
**Still undecided:** Discord access logic; the detail of the drop logic (both need the teach-vs-filter question revisited). What "ready" means for a drop: [interview pending]

## 8. The Realm (game) — its role
A browser-based whack-a-mole / space-invaders hybrid at https://kasdaededejoj.github.io/whack-a-mole/. It is a **qualification and initiation gate**: it filters the right people
into the Discord and the physical product before they get access. Status and code: `projects/the-realm-game.md` and `/HANDOVER.md`.
