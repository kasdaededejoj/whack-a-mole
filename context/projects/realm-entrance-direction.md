## Working interpretation

The trace provides direction; artifacts provide things worth examining; occasional signs of a presence alter the interpretation of what was found. Internal stage identifiers/checkpoints can remain while the visitor sees a continuous investigation. A transition should visibly follow from the preceding action and carry a mark, object, route or question into the next encounter. Merely hiding labels would not meet the linkage requirement. **9/10 — interpretation of the explicit direction, not an approved design specification.**

“Responsive mystery” is descriptive language here: an investigative action produces a perceptible, related change, revealing enough to make another action worth trying. It does not mean every object must animate, react constantly, or act as a character. **Proposal, not a technical or research term being asserted as established.**
