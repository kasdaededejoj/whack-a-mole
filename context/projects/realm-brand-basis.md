## Implications to explore — recommendations only

- Make the ordinary worth noticing. A familiar form with one precise anomaly could connect normality, curiosity and unconventionalism. This is a possible spatial interpretation, not a founder-defined translation of all three archetypes.
- Use investigation to change the meaning of another object or location. This carries forward Adam's required linkage between internal stages while preserving free exploration as an option under consideration.
- Explore proportions, hanging forms, layered planes, empty space and restrained motion as ways of translating the flowing vocabulary into angular low-poly geometry. These are compositional suggestions, not selected materials or approved assets.
- Keep the presence secondary and ambiguous. Hostility, surveillance, occult lore, ritual spaces and jump scares have not been selected by the first-person requirement or the brand's mystery.
- Assess whether an ordinary place and its anomalous detail carry the brand before adding more darkness, visual effects or symbols. The preceding courtyard, archive card, sealed room and passage examples remain proposals.

**8/10 — untested interpretation, consistent with the vizar'dd's recommendations; not new founder decisions.**
