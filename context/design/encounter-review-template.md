# Encounter review template — working aid

Moved out of the living design guide on 2026-10-21 after Adam distinguished design principles from activity-specific planning. This is an optional development aid, not a required scene structure, approved encounter, or fixed duration.

Use it for a storyboard, visual study, or prototype review when helpful. Record enduring decisions and discoveries in `../../BRAND_DESIGN.md`; keep the particular exercise here or in its own project note.

| Question | What to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival | What does the visitor see, hear, or recognize on entering? |
| Invitation | Which concrete feature gives them a reason to investigate? |
| Action | What can they try, and how do the available controls make that possible? |
| Response | What perceptible, related consequence follows their action? |
| Discovery | What changes in their understanding of the object or place? |
| Link | What trace, object, recognition, or consequence connects this encounter to another? |
| Return | If revisiting is possible, what remains recognizable and what has changed? |
| Recovery | What happens after a missed clue, repeated action, interrupted session, or unusable input? |

First-minute storyboards, visual studies, and observation exercises are possible ways to develop the design. None is a universal requirement for every artifact or scene, and no test threshold or production task is approved by listing them.
